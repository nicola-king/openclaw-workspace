### ARTICLE 7: INSPECTION & ACCEPTANCE
### 第七条：检验与验收

**Factory Inspection 工厂检验**:
- Buyer may inspect goods before shipment (at Buyer's cost)
- Seller shall provide 7 days notice before inspection
- Inspection covers: quantity, appearance, functionality

**Acceptance 验收**:
- Buyer shall inspect goods within 7 days after arrival
- Written notice of defects must be submitted within 24 hours
- Failure to notify = deemed accepted

**Third-Party Inspection 第三方检验**:
- ☐ SGS / ☐ BV / ☐ Intertek (if required)
- Cost borne by: ☐ Seller ☐ Buyer

---

### ARTICLE 8: CUSTOMS & DOCUMENTATION
### 第八条：报关与单证

**Export Customs (China) 出口报关 (中国)**:
- Seller responsible for export customs clearance
- Documents provided:
  - Commercial Invoice (3 originals + 3 copies)
  - Packing List (3 originals + 3 copies)
  - Bill of Lading
  - Certificate of Origin
  - Quality Certificate
  - [CE/AS/NZS Certification]

**Import Customs (Destination) 进口报关 (目的国)**:
- Buyer responsible for import customs clearance
- Buyer responsible for import duties, GST, taxes
- Seller provides necessary documents for clearance

---

### ARTICLE 9: RISK TRANSFER
### 第九条：风险转移

**Risk Transfer 风险转移**:
- FOB/CIF: Risk transfers when goods pass ship's rail at loading port
- EXW: Risk transfers at Seller's factory
- DDP: Risk transfers at Buyer's premises

**Insurance 保险**:
- ☐ Seller arranges marine insurance (110% of invoice value)
- ☐ Buyer arranges insurance
- Coverage: All Risks + War Risk + Strike Risk

---

### ARTICLE 10: INSTALLATION SUPPORT
### 第十条：安装支持

**Seller's Responsibility 卖方责任**:
- Provide detailed installation manual (English)
- Provide installation video tutorials
- ☐ Dispatch technician to site (if agreed)
  - Seller covers: salary, visa, travel insurance
  - Buyer covers: food, accommodation, local transport

**Buyer's Responsibility 买方责任**:
- Prepare foundation according to drawings
- Arrange local labor for installation
- Provide necessary tools and equipment
- Ensure site access and safety

---

### ARTICLE 11: FORCE MAJEURE
### 第十一条：不可抗力

**Force Majeure Events 不可抗力事件**:
- War, riots, strikes
- Natural disasters (earthquake, flood, typhoon)
- Government actions (export/import bans)
- Pandemics, epidemics
- Fire, explosion

**Notice 通知**:
- Affected party must notify within 7 days
- Performance extended by duration of force majeure
- If exceeds 90 days, either party may terminate

---

### ARTICLE 12: BREACH OF CONTRACT
### 第十二条：违约责任

**Seller's Breach 卖方违约**:
- Quality defects: repair/replace at Seller's cost
- Delivery delay: 0.1% per day (max 5%)
- Non-delivery: return double deposit

**Buyer's Breach 买方违约**:
- Payment delay: 0.1% per day
- Non-payment after 30 days: Seller may terminate
- Cancellation: forfeit deposit + 30% of contract value
