# Visual Prompts — 闪念配图

## Prompt 1: 深夜屏幕前的第一次

```
Cinematic, hyper-realistic, close-up of a laptop screen in a dark room,
a terminal window with white text on black background,
reflection of a person's face faintly visible in the glass,
moody blue light cast on the face,
shot on 35mm, deep shadows, minimalist, quiet tension
--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250
```

## Prompt 3: 十七次打开，第十七次笑了

```
Cinematic, hyper-realistic, wide shot of a person sitting in a cafe at dusk,
laptop open, condensation rings on the wooden table from a coffee cup,
outside the window street lights just turning on,
the person is leaning back, not typing, just looking at the screen,
warm golden hour light fading into blue hour,
shot on 35mm, contemplative, peaceful
--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250
```
