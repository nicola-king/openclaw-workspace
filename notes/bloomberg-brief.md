## One-Page Brief

### The Story

Chinese construction material exporters are quietly redirecting supply chains from tariff-burdened Western markets to the Middle East, where a $250B post-war reconstruction market is opening its first procurement wave.

### Why Now

Three simultaneous drivers:
1. **US 232 tariff on steel/aluminum raised to 50%** — making Chinese structural steel exports to the US uneconomical
2. **Middle East post-war reconstruction** — $250B market with first tenders Q3-Q4 2026
3. **Chinese policy support** — 9810 tax rebate upon departure + zero-tariff access to 53 African nations
