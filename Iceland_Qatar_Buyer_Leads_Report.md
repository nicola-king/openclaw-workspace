  6. Market Entry Strategy

6.1 Iceland Strategy

1. TARGET: Identify 1-2 Icelandic distributors for steel/building materials
2. PARTNER: Use Eimskip for logistics; work with local engineering firms for project specs
3. CERTIFICATION: CE marking (EU standard) is mandatory for most products
4. KEY DIFFERENTIATOR: Geothermal-resistant materials and seismic design sell at premium
5. TENDER: Monitor Rikiskaup.is for government infrastructure tenders
6. COMPETITION: EU suppliers dominate due to EEA proximity & trade agreement
   - Chinese advantage: Price competitiveness on steel structures/machinery
   - Chinese disadvantage: Longer lead times vs EU suppliers

6.2 Qatar Strategy

1. REGISTRATION: Register on Qatar e-Tender portal + Qatar Chamber
2. LOCAL AGENT: Mandatory for government contracts - choose established Qatari partner
3. TARGET SECTORS: Oil & gas (LNG expansion), infrastructure (Ashghal), real estate (Barwa)
4. CERTIFICATION: Qatari standards (QS), ISO, plus US/EU certifications preferred
5. KEY WIN FACTORS: Competitive pricing + financing terms + delivery reliability
6. COMPETITION: Strong from China (14.4% share), also US, Germany, Italy, India, Turkey
7. TIMELINE: Qatar National Vision 2030 creates sustained demand through 2030+
8. EVENTS: Attend Project Qatar (annual Doha exhibition) for networking
