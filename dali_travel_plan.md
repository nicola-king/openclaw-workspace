4. Extreme Budget Tips

(cid:127) Hostel Dorm Bed — Book a 4-6 person dorm for ~40/night. Save even more by asking for weekly rate.
(cid:127) Jizhao Nunnery FREE Lunch — Legendary free vegetarian meal. Be respectful, arrive before 11:30.
(cid:127) Cook at Hostel — North Gate Market has cheap fresh produce. Many hostels offer kitchen access.
(cid:127) Free Attractions Rule — Dali Old Town, Erhai lakeside, Xizhou, Jizhao, Dali University — all FREE.
(cid:127) Skip Paid Sites — Three Pagodas (75), Cangshan Cable Car (80-120), Butterfly Spring — skip them all.
(cid:127) Bring Water Bottle — Refill at hostel. Save 3-5/day on drinks.
(cid:127) Street Food is King — Mixian (rice noodles) 8-12, Ersi 6-8, Baba pie 3-5. Avoid restaurant sit-downs.
(cid:127) Bike Negotiation — Group of 5, negotiate from 30 to 20/day per bike.
(cid:127) Weather Check — May 18-22: 18-28°C, sunny. Pack light + one jacket for evenings.

5. Important Notes

(cid:127) Prices are estimates as of May 2026. Actual costs may vary.
(cid:127) May is pre-summer season — book hostel at least 3 days ahead.
(cid:127) Self-drive route: Chongqing ﬁ Zunyi ﬁ Guiyang ﬁ Kunming ﬁ Dali (~8h). Use navigation app for real-time tolls.
(cid:127) Train option: Chongqing West ﬁ Kunming (hard sleeper ~6h, ~100 RMB) + Kunming ﬁ Dali (high-speed 2h, ~110 RMB).
(cid:127) Bring student ID if applicable for discounts.
(cid:127) Plan B: If budget is too tight, consider cutting to 4 days and save one night of accommodation.
